Behind the Scenes

How We Process Our Products

Every product passes through a six-stage processing pipeline — ensuring export-grade quality, safety, and consistency at each step.

01

Field Harvesting & Selection

Products are hand-harvested at peak ripeness from verified partner farms. Only produce meeting strict pre-harvest criteria enters the supply chain.

02

Sorting & Size Grading

Harvested produce is sorted by size, weight, and visual quality through manual and mechanical grading. Damaged or irregular items are removed.

03

Washing & Pre-Treatment

All produce is cleaned with potable, food-grade water. Natural pre-treatment methods extend shelf life and maintain texture — no artificial preservatives.

04

Controlled Drying

Temperature and humidity-controlled drying achieves precise moisture targets while preserving flavour, aroma, and nutritional content required for international export.

05

Quality Inspection & QR Assignment

Every batch is tested for moisture content, colour, and contamination. A unique QR code is assigned, linking buyers to the complete processing history.

06

Export-Grade Packaging

Approved batches are sealed in food-safe packaging with tamper-evident seals and QR traceability labels — ready for international shipment with full documentation.
